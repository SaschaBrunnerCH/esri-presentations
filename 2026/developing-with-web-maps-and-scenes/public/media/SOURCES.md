# Asset provenance

All assets come from the supplied `2026/EUDTS 2026 - ArcGIS Online - Developing with Web Maps and Web Scenes .pptx`.

- `original/slide-001.webp` through `original/slide-135.webp`: full-slide 1920 × 1080 PNG exports from Microsoft PowerPoint on Windows, encoded as lossless WebP. Every decoded RGB pixel was compared with its PNG export. `original/pixel-checksums.json` records SHA-256 hashes of those decoded RGB pixels, in row-major order.
- `experience-builder.mp4`: byte-for-byte extraction of `ppt/media/media1.mp4`, embedded on original slide 52.

The deck retains the original Esri branding, copyright notices, Marktstammdatenregister data attribution and Thomas Réaubourg / Unsplash photo acknowledgment as shown in the source. No replacement visuals or rewritten slides were added.
